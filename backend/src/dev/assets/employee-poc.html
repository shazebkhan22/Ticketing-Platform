Cygnus Ticketing System

Proof of Concept — Overview for Employees

What This System Is

The Cygnus Ticketing System is a web application that replaces manual Excel-based tracking for IT support tickets and projects. It covers ticket logging (warranty, AMC, installation, office issues), project tracking, customer records, and activity history — all in one place, accessible from any browser.

Your Role: Employee

As an Employee, you have access to the day-to-day tools needed to log and work on tickets and projects. Access is scoped to what you're assigned, so your dashboard stays focused on your own work.

You canDetails
Add a ticketLog new support tickets with priority, status, call type, and repair location
View your dashboardSee tickets and projects relevant to you at a glance
Track "My Tickets"View and update tickets assigned to you
Monitor overdue itemsSee tickets that have crossed their expected resolution time
Work on projectsView projects you're assigned to and add remarks/updates

Some areas — user management, customer records, inventory, analytics, system settings, and full project administration — are limited to Admin accounts.

Logging In

  1. Go to the Cygnus Ticketing System login page (link provided by your administrator).
  2. Enter the username and password sent to you by email.
  3. Change your password immediately after your first login.

Why This Matters

  • No more lost or out-of-date Excel sheets — one shared source of truth.
  • Clear visibility into what's assigned to you and what's overdue.
  • Full history of remarks and updates on every ticket and project.
